# Tribute

This design exists because of the parallel Lilith Datura drew on 19 Oct 2026:

> the microkernel people are trying to move trust from “sandbox the running agent” to “prove the agent’s binary and capabilities before it gets a process,” while blockchain people have been trying to move trust from “trust the executor” to “cryptographically prove the computation was done correctly under the stated rules.”

Both are attempts to replace ambient trust with explicit, checkable evidence of what is allowed to happen.

The capability intersection gate and the signed `caps` block in this repo are the practical form of that idea applied to the HiveBrainComms job bus.

Thank you for the clear framing.
